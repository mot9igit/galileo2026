Features

Allows you to connect any sensors to the terminal, and freely control them. The terminal processes even the lowest level of the analog signal from the sensors without connecting an additional external resistor.

Finds data from two CAN buses at the same time. The terminal allows you to decrypt more than 13,000 parameters from the CAN bus using the J1939 protocol. You spend less time searching for the necessary information about the condition of the car.

The device has 4 mounting points for easy installation. The dimensions of the terminal are only 91x66.5x25.5 mm., so compact that it fits in the palm of your hand.
